# Privacy Policy

In brief, we:

1. Do not collect any personal information
2. Do not send any audio from your device to our servers
3. Do not share any personal information with third parties
4. Use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports, which you can disable from within the app

## Privacy Policy for ALTOMEDIA

At ALTOMEDIA, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains what information ALTOGFX collects and how it is used.

## Consent

By using ALTOGFX, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

## Information we collect

ALTOGFX does not require an account and does not ask for personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or address. Audio processed by the app stays on your device.

## Crash reports

ALTOGFX uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymised crash reports so that defects can be fixed. Crash reporting can be disabled from within the app's settings.

## Advertising

ALTOGFX does not display third party advertising.

## Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes take effect when they are published on this page.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, contact ALTOMEDIA through the app's store listing.
