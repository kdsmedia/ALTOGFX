## Full description (max 4000 characters)

ALTO EFECT turns your Android phone into a serious guitar pedalboard — with no internet, no account, and no subscription.

Nine pedal-style effects share one signal chain: WAH, COMP, DRIVE, DIST, CHORUS, PHASER, DELAY, REVERB, and CAB. Every pedal has a real on/off switch, status lamp, and potentiometer-style controls you can turn like hardware. Reorder pedals, dial in parameters, and save your sound as a preset.

KEY FEATURES
• 9 guitar effects: wah, compressor, overdrive, distortion, chorus, phaser, delay, reverb, and cabinet.
• 8 factory presets: Studio Clean, Blues Drive, Classic Rock, Metal Storm, Ambient Space, Funk Wah, Lead Sustain, Acoustic Warm.
• Potentiometer controls and pedal-style on/off switches.
• Flexible effect chain: add, remove, and reorder pedals.
• Save, load, and manage your own presets locally.
• Guitar input through the microphone/headset jack or a USB audio interface.
• Output to headphones, speakers, or an audio interface.
• Low-latency native audio engine (Oboe/AAudio) with LV2/LADSPA processing.
• Input/output level, noise gate, and hum filter controls.
• 100% offline. No INTERNET permission, ads, analytics, or in-app purchases.

HOW TO USE
1. Connect your guitar to an adapter/audio interface, or use the phone microphone input.
2. Connect headphones to avoid feedback.
3. Open ALTO EFECT, tap POWER, and allow microphone access.
4. Choose a preset, enable the pedals you want, and turn the knobs.
5. Save your favourite combination as a new preset.

NOTES
• Use headphones. Phone speakers can cause feedback.
• Latency depends on your device and audio adapter.
• Tone is affected by your guitar, cable, adapter, and headphones.
• This is an effects processor, not a physical amplifier. Start at low volume.
• Requires Android 7.0 or newer.

ALTO EFECT is made for guitarists who want to practise, capture ideas, and perform with a pedalboard that fits in their pocket.
