ALTO EFECT 1.0 (versionCode 1)

First Google Play release.

• 9 pedal-style guitar effects: WAH, COMP, DRIVE, DIST, CHORUS, PHASER, DELAY,
  REVERB, and CAB.
• 8 factory presets: Studio Clean, Blues Drive, Classic Rock, Metal Storm,
  Ambient Space, Funk Wah, Lead Sustain, Acoustic Warm.
• Potentiometer controls and pedal-style on/off switches.
• Flexible effect chain: add, remove, and reorder pedals.
• Save and manage local presets.
• Low-latency native audio engine with LV2/LADSPA processing.
• Input/output level, noise gate, and hum filter controls.
• 100% offline; no ads, analytics, account, or in-app purchases.

Note: use headphones to avoid feedback. Latency depends on your device and
audio adapter.
